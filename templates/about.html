About Us

Our Story

A journey that started with a single cup of coffee.

Raj Cafe was founded in 2020 by Rajesh Kumar, a coffee enthusiast who wanted to bring the authentic cafe experience to Jamshedpur. What started as a small corner shop has now grown into a beloved community space where people gather to enjoy great coffee and even better company.

We believe that coffee is more than just a drink—it's an experience. From the moment you walk through our doors, you're greeted with the aroma of freshly roasted beans and the warmth of our friendly staff.

Our beans are sourced from sustainable farms across India and roasted in-house to ensure the perfect cup every time. We take pride in our craft and are committed to quality, sustainability, and community.
